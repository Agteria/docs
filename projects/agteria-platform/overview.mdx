`agteria-platform` is the real backend and supporting infrastructure for a partner-scoped portal where Agteria partners view the current approved results and status for the problems being worked on for them. It is a monorepo containing the API, background workers, and the portal and admin web apps.

## Design direction

- Raw scientific artifacts (`.pdb` files, docking outputs) live in Google Cloud Storage.
- Partner, problem, run, and artifact metadata plus UI-facing summaries live in PostgreSQL.
- Ingestion is API-first: direct uploads to Cloud Storage with a manifest-based finalize step.
- Partners only see the **current approved run** for their own problems; old runs are retained internally for traceability.
