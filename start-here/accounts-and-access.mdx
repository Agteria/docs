Access is governed by the [Engineering Constitution](/how-we-work/overview), Articles IV and IX.

## How access works

- **Org owners** administer the organization (currently @rafey).
- All other engineering access flows through **one team: `@Agteria/engineering`**, which has write permission on every active repository. Direct individual repo grants are prohibited — onboarding and offboarding are a single team-membership change.
- The default organization member role is **Read**.
- **Two-factor authentication is required org-wide.** Accounts without 2FA cannot join.

## Onboarding steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable 2FA">
    Turn on two-factor authentication on your GitHub account — the org requirement blocks joining without it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the org invite">
    An owner adds you to `@Agteria/engineering`. That team membership is your entire permission grant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the constitution">
    Read the [Engineering Constitution](/how-we-work/overview) and the pages in How We Work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your first pull request">
    The `protect-main` ruleset — not a supervisor — guarantees an owner reviews it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
